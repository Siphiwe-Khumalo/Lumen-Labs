The day I knew

letter one

My Nosipho,

I don't think I ever told you properly about the moment I knew. We were on a video call, the connection was terrible, and the screen froze right in the middle of you laughing. Pixelated, blurry, completely frozen, and I still couldn't stop smiling at it. Oh, I thought. It's her.

People say you can't really fall for someone you haven't met in person. They've clearly never heard your voice at midnight, or waited for your name to pop up on their screen.

Since you came into my life, my phone has become my favourite place. Not because of the phone. Because of who's on the other side of it.

Forever yours, Siphiwe ♡

Across the distance

letter two

Sthandwa sami,

I know the distance is hard. There are nights I wish I could reach through the screen and just hold your hand. Some days a voice note isn't enough, and a goodnight text feels so small for how much I feel.

But here's what the distance doesn't know: it has never once made me love you less. If anything it's taught me how much I'm willing to wait for you, how patient my heart can be, and how a person can feel like home before you've ever stood in the same room.

Every call, every text, every late night we refuse to hang up is a step closer. I'm counting them all.

Forever yours, Siphiwe ♡

Things I don't say enough

letter three

Nosipho,

I'm proud of you. For the hard things you push through quietly. For how you keep showing up for the people you love, even when you're tired. For being exactly who you are in a world that's always asking people to be someone else.

You are beautiful, and I don't just mean your face (although, wow, even through a phone camera). I mean the way you think, the way you care, the way one phone call with you can turn my whole day around.

I'm sorry for the times I forget to say all this out loud. Consider this me saying it, forever, in writing.

Forever yours, Siphiwe ♡

For your hardest days

open when you need me

My love,

If you're reading this one because today is heavy, breathe. You don't have to be strong right now.

You have survived every single bad day so far. That's a perfect record. And on this one you're not alone. I might be far away, but I'm only ever one call away, and I'm not going anywhere.

Drink some water. Eat something nice. Then call me, even if you don't want to talk. We can stay on the line and be quiet together. I'll be here.

Tomorrow is a new page. I'll be on it.

Forever yours, Siphiwe ♡

The day we finally meet

letter five

Nosipho,

I think about it all the time. The first time I see you without a screen between us. I've rehearsed what I'll say a hundred times, and I already know I'll forget every word the second I see you.

I want to hear your laugh without any lag. I want to find out how tall you really are next to me. I want the first hug, the one we've been saving all this time, and I want it to last way too long.

And after that, I want every someday we've talked about on all those late night calls. Slow Sunday mornings. Road trips with bad snacks and good music. A whole life that started with a hello on a screen.

Until then, I'm counting down. It's you. It's always been you.

Forever yours, Siphiwe ♡
