The day I knew

letter one

My Nosipho,

I don't think I ever told you properly about the moment I knew. It wasn't loud. There were no fireworks. You just laughed at something small — the kind of laugh you do when you're trying not to — and something inside me went very quiet and very sure. Oh, I thought. It's her.

Since then I've noticed that the world is a little softer when you're in it. Traffic is shorter. Mondays are kinder. Even my bad days have a place to land.

Thank you for being that place.

Forever yours ♡

When you're far away

letter two

Sthandwa sami,

On the days we're apart, I find you everywhere anyway. In a song on the radio. In the smell of rain on hot ground. In the way the sky goes pink just before it gets dark, as if it's blushing because it's thinking of you too.

Distance is just a number of kilometres. It doesn't know anything about us. It doesn't know that I carry you with me — in my phone, in my chest, in every plan I make for the future.

Come home to me soon. And if you can't yet, read this again and know I'm counting the days.

Forever yours ♡

Things I don't say enough

letter three

Nosipho,

I'm proud of you. For the hard things you push through quietly. For how you keep showing up for the people you love, even when you're tired. For being exactly who you are in a world that's always asking people to be someone else.

You are beautiful — and I don't just mean your face (although, wow). I mean the way you think, the way you care, the way you make a room feel warmer just by walking into it.

I'm sorry for the times I forget to say all this out loud. Consider this me saying it, forever, in writing.

Forever yours ♡

For your hardest days

open when you need me

My love,

If you're reading this one because today is heavy — come here. Breathe. You don't have to be strong right now.

You have survived every single bad day so far. That's a perfect record. And on this one, you're not alone, because I'm right here, and I'm not going anywhere.

Drink some water. Eat something nice. Call me, even if you don't want to talk — we can just sit on the phone and be quiet together.

Tomorrow is a new page. I'll be on it.

Forever yours ♡

Our someday

letter five

Nosipho,

Sometimes I daydream about our someday. A small house with too many plants. Sunday mornings that start slow and stay slow. Road trips with bad snacks and good music. Growing older and somehow still laughing at the same silly jokes.

I don't know exactly what the future looks like. But I know who I want in it.

It's you. It's always been you.

Forever yours ♡
