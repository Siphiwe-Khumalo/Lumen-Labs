Morning Mist

The mountains wake up slowly,
wrapped in their soft grey shawl —
and I think of you at sunrise,
the gentlest thing of all.

The mist lifts off the valley
the way you lift my day;
not all at once, but surely,
till every cloud's away.

for the girl who is my sunrise

In the Forest

Among the old and patient trees,
where light falls through in gold,
I'd tell you all my secrets
if I had your hand to hold.

The roots go deep, the branches high,
they've stood here many years —
and that's how I will love you, girl:
through sunshine and through tears.

steady as the trees

Tides

The ocean keeps on coming back
no matter how it's pulled;
it kisses every grain of sand
as if the shore were gold.

So if you ever wonder,
across the miles and foam,
just watch the waves, Nosipho —
I'm finding my way home.

always coming back to you

Wildflower

You didn't grow in tidy rows
or bloom when you were told —
you're wild and bright and stubborn,
and braver than you know.

Of all the flowers in the field,
the ones that sway and spin,
my eyes go straight to you, my love —
the bloom I'm lost within.

my favourite flower

Count the Stars

I tried to count the stars one night
to see how much I care;
I ran out of the darkness
before I ran out there.

Tonight we share a single sky,
though miles keep us apart;
the moon above your window, love,
is shining on my heart.

So keep the sky, the moon, the lot —
they're far too small, it's true.
There's not a single universe
as wide as me and you.

goodnight, my love
