My promises to you

Written down so you can hold me to every one.

  1. To choose you — on the easy days and especially on the hard ones.
  2. To listen first, and to always try to understand.
  3. To make you laugh until your cheeks hurt, even over a bad connection.
  4. To celebrate your wins louder than you do.
  5. To be there through every storm, on the phone for now and right beside you soon.
  6. To keep closing the distance until there's none left.
  7. To love you honestly, gently, and completely.
♥

Ngiyakuthanda, Nosipho
